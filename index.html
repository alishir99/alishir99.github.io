About me

How hard can it be?

I came to Sweden in 2015 without speaking Swedish, and my English was very limited.

I learned the language in grades 7 to 9, then moved on to the Technology programme in upper secondary school. In my second year I told my English teacher I wanted to study at Chalmers. She was honest with me: the teaching there is in English, and with my English at the time, she would think twice about it.

In 2026 I graduated from Chalmers with a degree in Computer Science and Engineering.

That road left me with a habit I still have. When a problem looks hard, my first thought is not "I can't do this" but "how hard can it be?" Then I figure out how it works, learn what I need, and solve it. I care about details and the quality of what I deliver, and about the people I work with. A solution should not only be correct. It should be useful to the person using it.

It is also why I build things in my free time. Our team ran its Friday quiz in PowerPoint. Nobody asked me to fix it, but I thought it could be simpler, so I built Friday Quiz.
