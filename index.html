About me

How hard can it be?

2015

  1. Arrived in Sweden.

    No Swedish, and very little English.

  2. Started at Chalmers.

    In upper secondary school I told my English teacher I wanted to study there. She was honest: the teaching is in English, and with my English at the time she would think twice.

  3. First summer at Volvo.

    I rebuilt a schematic tool and wrote the validation that made the team trust it. It replaced a manual workflow costing about 400 hours a year.

  4. Graduated from Chalmers.

    M.Sc. in Complex Adaptive Systems. My thesis at Volvo predicts truck vibration in seconds instead of hours.

That road left me with a habit I still have. When a problem looks hard, my first thought is not "I can't do this" but "how hard can it be?" Then I figure out how it works, learn what I need, and solve it. I care about details and the quality of what I deliver, and about the people I work with. A solution should not only be correct. It should be useful to the person using it.

It is also why I build things in my free time. Our team ran its Friday quiz in PowerPoint. Nobody asked me to fix it, but I thought it could be simpler, so I built Quizmys.
